# BitOfTech Expense Policy

Version 4.1, effective 1 January 2026. Applies to all employees and contractors.

## 1. General rules

1. Claim in the currency you actually paid in. Finance converts to GBP using the published
   reference rate for the date the expense was incurred, not the date you filed the report.
2. Submit a report within 30 days of the earliest expense on it. Reports submitted after
   30 days need written approval from a director.
3. Attach a receipt for every line over 75.00 GBP, or the equivalent in the currency claimed.
4. One report per trip or per calendar month. Do not mix a trip with routine monthly costs.

## 2. Travel

1. Book economy class for any flight under six hours.
2. Premium economy is allowed for flights over six hours with manager approval in advance.
3. Rail travel is reimbursed at standard class. First class is allowed only where it costs
   less than the standard class fare on the day.
4. Airport transfers, taxis and ride hailing are reimbursed in full where public transport is
   impractical or unsafe.

## 3. Accommodation

1. The limit is 180.00 GBP per night including tax, for all locations.
2. London, Zurich, New York, San Francisco, Tokyo and Singapore carry a higher limit of
   260.00 GBP per night.
3. Anything above the limit needs manager approval before the booking is made, and the
   approval must be referenced in the report.
4. Room service, minibar, spa and hotel laundry are not reimbursable.

## 4. Meals

1. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 60.00 GBP per person per day.
2. Client entertainment is reimbursed up to 90.00 GBP per person, and the report must name
   everyone who attended and the client they represent.
3. Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of client entertainment.
4. Any meal line over 150.00 GBP must be itemised per person.

## 5. Training and certification

1. Exam fees, certification renewals and course fees are reimbursable with manager approval.
2. A failed exam is reimbursed once. Further attempts are at your own cost.
3. Conference tickets are treated as training and follow the same rule.

## 6. Communications and equipment

1. Mobile plans are reimbursed up to 55.00 GBP per month for employees on a call rota.
2. Home broadband is not reimbursable. It is covered by the monthly remote working allowance
   and must not appear on an expense report.
3. Peripherals such as keyboards, mice and headsets go through IT procurement, not expenses.

## 7. What gets rejected

Reports are commonly rejected for these reasons:

1. A line that breaches a stated limit with no prior approval referenced.
2. Home broadband, or any other cost already covered by the remote working allowance.
3. Missing receipts on lines over 75.00 GBP.
4. Client entertainment with no attendee list.
5. Mixing a trip with routine monthly costs on one report.
